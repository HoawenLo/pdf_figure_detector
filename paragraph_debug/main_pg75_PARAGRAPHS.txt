17.5 Cylindricity specification
The toleranced feature is an integral feature. The nature and shape of the nominal toleranced feature is
explicitly given as a cylindrical surface, which is an areal feature.
In Figure 102 , the extracted cylindrical surface shall be contained between two coaxial cylinders with
a difference in radii of 0,1.
