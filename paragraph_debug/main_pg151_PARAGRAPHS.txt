Relations and dimensions of graphical symbols

--- BLOCK 3 ---
To harmonize the sizes of the symbols specified in this document with those of the other inscriptions
on the drawing (dimensions, letters, tolerances), the rules given in this annex, which are in accordance
with ISO 81714-1, shall be observed. Further graphical symbols are given in ISO 3098-5.
The graphical symbols described in Table 2 shall be drawn in accordance with Figures F.1 to F.5.
