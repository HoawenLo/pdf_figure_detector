--- BLOCK 8 ---
17.11.1 General
The toleranced feature can be an integral feature or a derived feature. The nature of the nominal
toleranced feature is a linear feature, a set of linear features, or an areal feature. The shape of each
nominal toleranced feature is explicitly given as a straight line or a flat surface. If the indicated feature
is a nominally flat surface and the toleranced feature is a set of straight lines in that surface, an
intersection plane indicator shall be indicated. The TED angles that are locked between the nominal
toleranced feature and the datums shall be defined by implicit TEDs (90°).
