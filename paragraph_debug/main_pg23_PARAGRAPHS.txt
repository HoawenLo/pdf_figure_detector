8 Geometrical specification indication

--- BLOCK 2 ---
8.1 General
A geometrical specification indication consists of a tolerance indicator, optional plane and feature
indications and optional adjacent indications. See Figure 11.

--- BLOCK 3 ---
Key
a tolerance indicator
b plane and feature indicator
c adjacent indications

Figure 11 — Elements of a geometrical specification indication

--- BLOCK 5 ---
The geometrical specification indication shall be connected to the leader line by a reference line.
The reference line shall be attached to the mid-point of the left hand end or the right hand end of the
tolerance indicator, if there is no optional plane and feature indication. If there are optional plane and
feature indication(s), the reference line shall be attached to the mid-point of the left hand end of the
tolerance indicator or the mid-point of the right hand end of the last plane and feature indicator. This
applies both in 2D and 3D.

--- BLOCK 6 ---
8.2 Tolerance indicator
The requirements shall be indicated in a rectangular frame which is divided into two or three sections.
The third, optional, datum section may consist of one to three compartments. The sections are always
arranged from left to right as in Figure 12.
