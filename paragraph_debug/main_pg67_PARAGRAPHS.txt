When a direction feature is indicated as shown in Figure 85 b) or implied as in Figure 85 c), the width of
the tolerance zones is defined by an infinite set of straight segments, inclined in the direction indicated
by the direction feature indicator (see Figure 86). Each of these segments has a length equal to the
tolerance value and has its midpoint located on the theoretical shape of the tolerance zone by default.
