Figure 54 — All around interpretation

--- BLOCK 6 ---
The workpiece has to be relatively simple for an “all around” indication to be unambiguous. For example,
if there were a vertical hole in the middle of the workpiece shown in Figure 51 and Figure 53, it is not
clear whether the specification would apply to the surface of the hole or not. In Figure 51 and Figure 53,
the collection plane can be any plane parallel to datum A. Depending on where the collection plane is
located, it may or may not intersect the hole. In such cases, the all around indication shall not be used.
