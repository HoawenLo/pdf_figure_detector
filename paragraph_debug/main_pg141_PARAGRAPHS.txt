--- BLOCK 3 ---
The convex hull filter is a long-pass filter. It removes almost all the details in the profile and considers only
the highest peaks. In this case, the resulting filtered profile also has a total height range of about 25 µm.
Figure E.5 shows a different surface profile. Like the profile in Figure E.1 it is 50 mm long and has a
total height range of slightly more than 40 µm, but the structure of the surface is different.
